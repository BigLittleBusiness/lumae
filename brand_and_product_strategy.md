# Lumae — Brand and Product Strategy

## Positioning

**Lumae** is the proposed brand for an Australian and New Zealand customer-feedback platform that turns each signal into a clearly owned next step. The name combines the ideas of *lumen* (clarity) and *luma* (light) without borrowing the visual codes of any incumbent. Its recommended descriptive line is **Customer clarity, in action.**

The platform should initially lead with relationship-led, digitally mature ANZ businesses rather than claim a broad market. Financial services presents the most compelling compliance and service-recovery wedge; healthcare provides a high-value governance-led use case; retail offers the clearest omnichannel measurement problem; and professional services and B2B SaaS create a pragmatic early-adopter segment. The opportunity hypothesis is evidence-based but should be validated through 15–20 buyer interviews before a full product build.

> **Brand promise:** Know what customers feel. Know what to do next.

## Visual Identity

The brand direction takes account of ANZ buyers’ need for confidence in regulated, service-heavy categories and the high information density of feedback analytics. It uses a dark ink foundation for seriousness, an unmistakably blue-green action colour instead of incumbent-SaaS cobalt, and a restrained warm signal to make feedback states human and clear. The direction is intentionally not a reproduction of any ANZ bank or SaaS brand.

| Element | Specification | Intended role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary colour | **Harbour Ink** — `#10283B` | Authority, navigation, headings, and data-dense surfaces. |
| Action colour | **Tidal Teal** — `#0E867E` | Primary CTAs, positive momentum, and key interaction states. |
| Highlight colour | **Signal Coral** — `#E96E59` | Detractor/attention cues and small high-value highlights. |
| Warm neutral | **Salt Paper** — `#FBFAF7` | Page backdrop; gives the brand a more editorial, less templated feel. |
| Cool neutral | **Mist** — `#E9F0F0` | Secondary panels and visual relief. |
| Typography | **Manrope** for interface and headings; **DM Mono** for response/metric details. | Clear, contemporary, high-legibility product language with a precise data layer. |

The logo is a compact, asymmetric conversation-and-signal mark: three growing response arcs contained by an open, rounded form. The open edge indicates that feedback is a continuing dialogue, while its controlled geometry suits an enterprise product. The wordmark uses a custom-weight, lower-case rendering of **lumae** for a calm, sophisticated feel. Maintain a clear space equal to the diameter of the smallest internal signal circle; never compress, outline, recolour, or place the full-colour mark on low-contrast imagery.

## Product Blueprint

The future platform should be designed around a short path from **collect → understand → recover → improve**. Phase-one functionality should support B2B service teams without requiring a complex enterprise deployment.

| Area | Primary pages | Phase-one functions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Workspace | Home dashboard; response feed; saved views | NPS/CSAT/CES trend, response volume, benchmark-ready segments, detractor alerts, and clear ownership of next actions. |
| Survey studio | Survey library; builder; preview | NPS, CSAT, CES and custom questions; brand themes; logic; mobile-first preview; multilingual-ready structure; email/SMS/in-app/QR distribution. |
| Journey automation | Journeys; triggers; delivery health | Event-triggered and scheduled sends; frequency safeguards; audience exclusions; delivery and response-status monitoring. |
| Response intelligence | Response detail; tags; action queue | Sentiment and themes; filters; account, location and journey context; comments; closed-loop assignments and escalation. |
| Analysis | Trends; segments; reports; exports | Score trends, response rate, driver views, date comparisons, saved filters, stakeholder-ready reports, and export. |
| Integrations | Integrations directory; connection settings | CRM/helpdesk primitives: HubSpot, Salesforce, Zendesk, Intercom and API/webhook architecture, subject to verified vendor capabilities. |
| Administration | Organisation; brands; people; roles; billing; security | White-label brand control, role-based access, audit logs, retention settings, consent text, and billing administration. |

The early product should not over-promise automation. Delivery and simple workflow rules can be implemented in the managed application environment. High-frequency polling, persistent queue workers, or real-time integrations should be assessed before implementation and use an always-available service only if their operating needs require it.

## Commercial Model

The proposed price architecture is deliberately transparent in **AUD** and positioned below enterprise suites while preserving a credible path to multi-team usage. Annual values are calculated from the discounted monthly equivalent and should be validated through buyer discovery and willingness-to-pay tests.

| Plan | Ideal customer | Monthly | Annual billing | Included allowance | Core value |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| **Signal** | Small teams establishing a feedback cadence | A$89/month | A$948/year, equivalent to A$79/month | 1,000 responses/month; 3 full seats | NPS & CSAT builder, email, dashboard, exports, basic segments. |
| **Momentum** | Small-to-medium teams operationalising feedback | A$249/month | A$2,628/year, equivalent to A$219/month | 5,000 responses/month; 10 full seats | Everything in Signal, plus SMS/in-app distribution, automations, CRM/helpdesk integrations, response actions, and scheduled reports. |
| **Clarity** | Medium businesses managing multiple locations, brands, or journeys | A$549/month | A$5,748/year, equivalent to A$479/month | 20,000 responses/month; 25 full seats | Everything in Momentum, plus multi-brand controls, white-label surveys, advanced roles, API access, priority onboarding, and enterprise reporting. |

Overage pricing, SMS pass-through charges, security review, SSO, and custom data-residency requirements should remain quote-led until operating costs and integration effort are verified. A 14-day trial is appropriate for Signal and Momentum; Clarity should offer a guided pilot instead of an unassisted trial.

## Brand and Domain Decision

Use **Lumae** as the working brand, with `lumae.com.au` as the first domain candidate and `lumae.co.nz` or `lumae.cx` as secondary candidates. Availability, trade-mark clearance, and a formal legal review must occur before committing to the name, visual identity, or paid media.
