# ANZ B2B SaaS Visual-Language Review

## Observational review, 14 August 2026

The brand system is informed by a focused review of two established ANZ SaaS leaders, not copied from either. Xero’s Australian site uses a direct small-business promise, plain language, outcome-led plan segmentation, high-contrast calls to action, substantial whitespace, and local currency. Culture Amp’s current site couples a more editorial hero treatment with a restrained navigation system, human-centred photography, and a strong “insight to action” narrative. Both establish that regional buyers respond to clarity, visible commercial framing, accessible hierarchy, and a credible path from information to operational action. [1] [2]

Lumae responds to that evidence with an original identity: Harbour Ink conveys the confidence required by financial services and healthcare; Tidal Teal sets an independently recognisable action state rather than adopting a common cobalt SaaS default; Salt Paper creates calm, premium contrast for high-density analytics; and Signal Coral is reserved for customer-risk or attention cues. Manrope’s generous x-height and tabular-friendly figures suit dense dashboards, while DM Mono visibly separates metrics and response metadata from the explanatory interface copy. The typography decision is product-led and original, not a claim that any observed brand uses these exact typefaces.

Domain availability and trade-mark clearance are intentionally outside this design review. **Lumae is a working name only** until formal clearance and registration checks are completed.
