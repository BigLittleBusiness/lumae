# Source Verification Notes

## Competitor pricing pages verified on 14 August 2026

| Provider | Verified current public finding | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Qualtrics | The self-service Strategic Research plan is displayed at **US$420 per month, billed annually (US$5,040 per year)** for 1,000 responses shared across unlimited users. Enterprise pricing is custom and interaction-based, for 5,000+ responses. | https://www.qualtrics.com/buy-online/ |
| SurveyMonkey | The location-served pricing page displayed Team plan pricing in **SGD**, with a starting rate of **SGD 49 per user per month**, 3-user minimum, annual billing, and 50,000 responses per year. Enterprise is presented as sales-led for 5+ users with a custom response limit. Pricing currency and presentation can be localisation-dependent and must be labelled accordingly in the website. | https://www.surveymonkey.com/pricing/ |
| Medallia | Medallia uses annual-tier pricing based on Experience Data Records and does **not** publish monthly or annual dollar figures. Its model incorporates experience signals, analytics, workflows, security, unlimited users, and role-based reporting. | https://www.medallia.com/pricing/ |
| AskNicely | AskNicely presents Learn, Grow, and Transform tiers starting from 500 responses and scaling with response volume. It primarily contracts annually, publishes no plan-price figures on this page, does not offer a free trial, and lists SSO as a **US$1,500 annual** add-on. | https://www.asknicely.com/pricing |
| Delighted | Qualtrics’ official successor page states that Delighted is **no longer available** and directs prospective users to Qualtrics Customer Feedback Software. It should remain in the comparison as a historical named platform with no current purchasable price, not as an active vendor. | https://www.qualtrics.com/delighted/ |

## ANZ viability evidence verified on 14 August 2026

| Segment | Verified signal | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Financial services | The Australian Financial Complaints Authority reported **111,373 complaints in 2025**, up **14%** year on year, and **A$643 million** in compensation/refunds, up **120%**. It highlighted a need for more consistent, accessible, customer-focused outcomes. This supports a compliance-aware, closed-loop feedback and complaint-management opportunity. | https://www.afca.org.au/news/media-releases/afca-receives-record-number-of-complaints-in-2025-calendar-year |
| Healthcare | ABS reported that in 2024–25, **26.6%** of people delayed or did not see a GP when needed, **26.0%** felt their GP wait was longer than acceptable, **26.4%** felt the same about specialist waits, and **22.5%** used telehealth. These service-access and journey-friction signals support event-based patient-experience measurement, not generic NPS alone. | https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/health/health-services/patient-experiences/latest-release |
| Professional services and SaaS/technology | ABS reported that **46%** of Australian businesses were innovation-active in 2024–25, **12%** used AI (up from 1% in 2022–23), and **34%** used ICT to improve responsiveness to customer needs. Information, Media and Telecommunications had the highest AI use at **38%**. This supports early targeting of digitally mature service businesses and technology firms. | https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/industry/technology-and-innovation/characteristics-australian-business/latest-release |
| Retail | ABS reported Australia’s online share of retail sales increased from **6.3% in 2019 to 11.4% in 2024**. The channel shift supports an omnichannel feedback proposition across web, delivery, store, support, and returns journeys. | https://www.abs.gov.au/articles/retail-trade-journey-through-75-years-retail-statistics |

The final competitor comparison will show publicly verified pricing only, identify its currency and billing basis, and mark sales-led/undisclosed figures explicitly. It will not convert currency or infer an annual price where a public source does not state one.
